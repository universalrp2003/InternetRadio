Internet Radio streams radio stations over the internet with Media3/ExoPlayer: tap a station
and it plays, keep browsing the list while it plays, and control it from the notification or
the lock screen after you leave the app.

* Background playback with a media notification (play/pause, next/previous).
* A station list you can search, with favourites so the ones you like are one tap away.
* Handles the usual stream formats (MP3, AAC, HLS) and reports a clear message when a station
  is offline instead of pretending to play.

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. The app uses the internet only to play the station you
chose; nothing about you is collected or sent anywhere.
