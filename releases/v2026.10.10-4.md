# Live Guard v2.30 (Release v2026.10.10-4)

### New in this release:
- **Colored Quality Indicator in Status Bar Pill**: Added the network transfer arrows (`↑↓`) directly inside the floating status bar pill right beside the mobile data count (e.g. `↑↓ 14.4 MB`).
- **Dynamic Quality Colors**:
  - **Flashing Gold**: Top network quality (high transfer rate or <55ms ping & low jitter).
  - **Emerald Green**: Good/medium network quality.
  - **Blinking Red**: Poor network quality or high packet latency.
- **Pack Alert Styling**: Text transitions to amber/red and flashes when data pack quota is at or above 75% and 90%.
