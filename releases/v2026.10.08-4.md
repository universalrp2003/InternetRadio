# CleanSweep v2.17 & Updates (2026.10.08-4)

### CleanSweep v2.17 Highlights
- **Reliable & Instant Tamil Spoken Charger Announcement**:
  - Pre-initializes Android TextToSpeech engine directly in `SystemEventWatcher` background thread.
  - Eliminated deadlocks and reduced connection stabilization pause from 1.5s to 0.6s.
  - Shortened plug-in deduplication cooldown from 45s to 15s.
  - Guarantees immediate Tamil spoken alert upon cable insertion.
- **Upgraded Multi-Source News Strip**:
  - Expanded feeds beyond BBC Tamil to cover **Tamil Nadu** (தினத்தந்தி), **India** (News18 Tamil, தினமணி, புதிய தலைமுறை), **International** (BBC Tamil, Oneindia), and **Cybersecurity Safety Alerts** (CERT-In).
  - Implemented history tracking to cycle unseen headlines first and prevent showing the same news repeatedly.
  - Added dedicated refresh button right on the news strip for instant manual updates.
- **Home Screen Widget Touch Crash Prevention**:
  - Tapping the widget body now instantly refreshes widget state and battery/storage stats without opening the app, preventing launcher crashes.
  - Opening the app is strictly reserved for tapping "Clean".
- **Wi-Fi Intruder & New Device Detection**:
  - Checkmark toggle for user-verified known devices on the network scan screen.
  - Automatic Wi-Fi scan on app launch (with toggle switch to turn on/off).
  - Immediate spoken voice and notification alerts in Tamil whenever an unrecognized device connects.
- **On-Device Passive App Network & Tracker Monitor**:
  - Added live socket connection inspection (`/proc/net/tcp`, `/proc/net/tcp6`) without battery drain or VPN icons.
  - Attributes outward connections to installed apps, maps IP addresses to cloud hosts/companies (Google, Meta, AWS, Cloudflare, etc.), and flags known telemetry and advertising trackers.
