# Live Guard / CleanSweep 2.37 — automatic voice, today's news and a clearer home widget

## Changes requested by the owner

The owner reports **manual speech is audible but automatic alerts are silent** on
Redmi 13 5G; screenshots show Android 16/API 36, with the exact ROM unspecified.
The confirmed choices are: redesign the Android **launcher/home-screen widget**,
show larger **battery % and watts together**, and admit only news **published today
in the phone's local timezone**. The floating pill is not the redesign target.

### Automatic speech: delivery fixes and honest diagnostics

- Speech-engine creation/usage is serialized on the main thread. Initialization
  failure/timeouts, language/voice availability, the return from `speak`, and real
  start/done/error callbacks are checked. A bounded queue avoids automatic alerts
  flushing one another; explicit manual reads may interrupt. Each request retains
  its own language. An available installed offline voice in the **same language**
  can be retried once after an online-voice error/timeout; offline data is not promised.
- Power broadcasts schedule short, gated jobs that await engine completion rather
  than finishing while speech initializes. These are independent of card/pill
  switches and do not silently enable a permanent monitor. Duplicate jobs for the
  same connection are kept once; obsolete or over-two-minute power events are skipped.
  Plug-in deduplication and low/full/heat/storage/health cooldowns are committed
  when the engine reports a start, not merely when a request is accepted.
- Runtime power, battery and Wi-Fi/mobile receivers/listeners operate while the app
  is visible, the existing enabled monitor or its visual-monitor fallback job runs.
  Manifest power broadcasts alone can be restricted/deferred on Android8+; without
  an active owner a power alert can be missed before any job is scheduled. No instant
  cold-background guarantee is claimed. Registration status is visible in Voice.
  Duplicate visual jobs no longer replace one another; fallback continuation is chained. Hourly checks are fallback observations,
  not instant-switch detection. Master/event switches and quiet hours still apply.
  Early-unplug speech also requires the configured earliest hour and a level below 90%.
- **Voice & daily watch** shows the last automatic event/stage/time and current
  language/volume evidence, offers **Test automatic charging voice** with the real
  charging-category/quiet-hour gates, and a guarded TTS-settings route. The test is
  not a simulated physical connection. A manual test bypasses quiet hours, not the
  master switch. Queued, blocked, rejected and engine-finished are distinct outcomes.
- An engine start/completion is **not proof that sound was heard**. Media volume,
  DND, Bluetooth routing, Android job quotas/Doze and OEM background restrictions
  can still prevent or delay audible delivery; force-stop prevents background work.
  “Louder” affects utterance amplitude only, not system volume or silent/DND policy.

### News only, with a real publication date

- RSS/Atom publication timestamps, including year/timezone, must place a Tamil
  headline in **today's phone-local day** and not in the future. Missing dates,
  update-only dates, yesterday/two-day-old items, static safety tips and synthetic
  “today” fallback items are excluded. Still-today cached items can remain offline.
- Normalize titles and canonical article URLs to deduplicate. Persist a bounded
  day/timezone-scoped seen set; automatic rotation advances through unseen items
  and stops when caught up instead of looping forever. Refresh does not reset it.
  The date/timezone is re-evaluated after fetching and on visible Home refreshes.
- No publisher/category ribbon in the news banner. Source/URL/publication metadata
  is retained internally; review, explicit read-aloud and open-article actions remain.
  Empty, offline and feed-unavailable states are explicit—no old-news substitution.
- Parsing/network work is off the UI thread, with bounded input/concurrency, a
  shared fetch budget and socket timeouts; external XML entities/DTDs are rejected.
  Feed availability/reporting is not independently verified and can vary.

### Assistant identity and evidence

- “What AI API is this?”, model/provider identity and equivalent local identity
  questions are answered from public app configuration **without an API call/web search**.
  A configured provider/model is not proof that model is available or ran; Free
  mode can use different endpoints, identified by individual reply metadata.
- Other web evidence is filtered for title/query relevance and deduplicated by
  canonical URL. Unrelated first-hit Wikipedia fallback is removed. Excerpts are
  collapsible and labelled as fetched evidence, **not independently verified facts**.
  Local identity answers follow the AI answer-language choice, not the Home language.

### Launcher widget and trustworthy precision

- A larger, resizable **4×4 target** widget defaults to battery **% + battery-side W**,
  with per-widget **% + W / W / %** view cycling. A narrow fine-resolution reading
  can stack the measurements rather than trim real reported digits. Useful available
  temperature, signed current, voltage, mobile usage, storage and snapshot time are
  secondary; compact sizes hide secondary rows. A saved daily brief is dated and
  shown only at sufficient height, not presented as a current security verdict.
- **Clean**, **Details** and **Refresh** actions have distinct widget-aware intents.
  Queries/refreshes use coalesced background snapshots, not main-thread sensor/stats work.
  No new permanent refresh service is enabled; Android controls launcher update timing.
- Additional battery decimals, at most five, appear **only if the actual battery
  broadcast reports a finer level/scale**. Ordinary integer-resolution phones show
  integer percent; no animation, estimated-capacity interpolation or invented `77.45678%`.
  Watts use two display decimals, not an adapter-output/accuracy claim.
- Android public current properties are treated as **microamps as specified**, not
  multiplied using a magnitude guess that inflated low-current readings. OEM sensor
  units/calibration can still be wrong. Charging power requires an actively charging,
  finite/plausible reading; unavailable/full/paused/unplugged wattage is `— W`, not fake zero.
- Fractional broadcast levels cannot be rounded into a falsely exact observed
  20→80 history interval. Legacy integer observations remain supported.

## Preserved

The wrapping/tappable **Estimated phone age** badge remains on active Home, labelled
as a coarse estimate with unsupported evidence unavailable. All six 2.35 improvements
remain: truthful dated unknowns, allowlisted compatibility diagnostics, opt-in bounded
security history, observed charging/charts, conditional data budgeting/history, and
manually reviewed grounded checklists/fresh rechecks. Histories still default off.
Targeted settings, all-current-issues Ask AI and privacy choices are retained.
The separate floating pill stays watts-only while connected and restores its normal
unplugged view with opted-in monitoring. Same package/signing key; API 26 minimum.
Ramesh Radio **1.8**, PulseEQ **1.2** and both legacy modules are unchanged in source.
