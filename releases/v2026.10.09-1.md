# Android app updates — v2026.10.09-1

### CleanSweep v2.20
* **Wi-Fi Scanner Accuracy**: Phantom device detections have been resolved by strictly requiring positive TCP socket responses, ARP MAC table entries, or valid LAN hostnames before reporting a host as online.
* **Passive App Network & Telemetry Tracker**: Inspects live active socket connections and data transfers locally without needing a VPN service or VPN permission dialog. Directly attributes network traffic to applications and companies.
* **Smart Security Alerts Filtering**: Curated Tamil cybersecurity alerts and general headlines, filtering out normal Android OS, Qualcomm, Xiaomi, Google, and default messaging services to prevent false alarms.
* **Direct AI Assistant Bar**: Direct question bar from the home screen opens the conversation interface immediately upon asking.
* **Louder Voice Alerts & Persistent Monitor**: Optional setting to increase alert volume when speaking, and persistent background battery monitoring.
