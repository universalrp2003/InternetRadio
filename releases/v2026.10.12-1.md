## What 2.41 (code 46) changes

Four owner-requested changes on top of the published 2.40 core, and no other behaviour change:

1. **Honest watts/amps.** The charging numbers on the notification card and the battery card
   are named for what they are — **battery-side** measurements (`V_batt × I_batt`, the only
   charging number Android exposes) — and are shown only when the phone actually reported
   them. Amps are no longer substituted where watts belong, current is always shown as a
   labelled magnitude with its own unit, and a missing sensor stays "Not reported" instead of
   becoming `0.00 W`. Implausible kernel values (voltage outside 2–12 V, current beyond 20 A,
   watts beyond 150 W, non-finite spikes) are refused. One shared policy
   (`data/ChargingHonestyPolicy.kt`) computes the reading used by the battery reader, so the
   card, the health screen and the pill cannot disagree.
2. **Screen-on/off charging note.** While charging, the card and the battery screen say
   plainly that the screen's own draw changes what the battery-side figure reads — and that
   the figure is measured at the battery, never the charger adapter's output. A measurement
   note, not a fault and not a claim about the charger.
3. **AI output-budget + provider model check.** Every generation ask carries a bounded
   output-token budget (32 minimum for a real answer, 4 000 maximum; the model list keeps
   zero). A request whose own deadline is nearly spent asks for a *shorter* answer instead of
   being handed an open-ended budget and timing out. After "Load models" the app also compares
   the saved model with the provider's own `/models` list: a model the provider does not
   contain is rejected with the existing translatable message instead of producing a doomed
   request, and the same check runs before the connection test. An unavailable list is never
   treated as evidence against the model, and no model is silently substituted.
4. **44 °C heat warning.** The battery heat threshold is **44 °C** (45 °C in the voice path and
   42 °C in one plug-in check before — two numbers for one thing). A single policy
   (`data/ThermalWarningPolicy.kt`) decides the warning, names the sensor honestly (battery vs
   CPU zone), formats the temperature, and hushes a repeat for 3 hours. The app never claims a
   cause, never calls the phone damaged, and never invents a temperature when no sensor
   reported one.

Both languages cover every new string (941 audited EN/TA catalog keys, 931 before 2.41).

## What 2.41 does not do

- It does not re-add notification/message reading, accessibility, contacts, SMS or call-log
  access. The retired 2.39 reader stays removed and archived reference-only.
- It does not disable, bypass or weaken Play Protect, rename the package, change the signing
  identity or hide the installation source.
- It does not claim that the 44 °C figure is a diagnosis, that the charging watts are the
  charger's output, or that a model list proves a key/account/quota will work.

## Installation

Install `CleanSweep-v2.41.apk` with **Play Protect left ON**, from the normal download route,
over your existing app — do not uninstall first. Do not use adb or a modified build. The
published APK is the exact device-tested bytes; a different digest is a different build and is
not covered by the recorded device test.
