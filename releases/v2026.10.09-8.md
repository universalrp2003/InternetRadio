# Android app updates — v2026.10.09-8

### CleanSweep v2.27
* **Auto-Start On Phone Reboot**: Added `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` and `BootReceiver` so CleanSweep's ongoing status monitoring, widgets, and health services restore seamlessly when the phone restarts.
* **OEM Auto-Start Settings Shortcut**: Added a one-tap shortcut in Settings to open Xiaomi (MIUI / HyperOS), Oppo, Vivo, or Samsung Autostart Management screen so OEM battery killers do not terminate boot broadcasts.
* **Fixed DataStore Singleton Crash**: Resolved `IllegalStateException: There are multiple DataStores active for the same file` by making `SettingsRepo.store` a thread-safe singleton.
* **Polished Status Bar & Mobile Data Display**: Verified that status bar pill, widgets, and home-screen mobile pack card reflect live accurate usage without duplication.
