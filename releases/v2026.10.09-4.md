# Android app updates — v2026.10.09-4

### CleanSweep v2.23
* **Variable Data Pack Entry**: Enter any custom recharge pack size in GB (e.g. 1.5 GB, 3 GB, 6 GB, 12 GB, 25 GB, 50 GB) with interactive modal dialogues in the Mobile & Data screen.
* **Strict Mobile Cellular Tracking**: Strictly isolates cellular network traffic (`TYPE_MOBILE`) so Wi-Fi traffic is never counted towards the pack or daily mobile tally.
* **Prominent Widget Mobile Data Readout**: Clean home-screen widget showing Today's Mobile Data prominently formatted in GB/MB (`106.0 MB Today` or `1.45 GB Today`) with complete pack breakdown (`Pack: 106.0 MB / 12 GB (11.89 GB left)`).
* **Polished Notification Bar**: Real mobile data usage displayed at the top of the ongoing notification card with clean pack quota, remaining balance, and secondary charging metrics.
* **Progress Bar & Reset Triggers**: Visual consumption bar for pack quota and instant one-tap reset when a new recharge begins.
