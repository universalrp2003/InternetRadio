# CleanSweep v2.16 & Updates (2026.10.08-3)

### CleanSweep v2.16 Highlights
- **Maximized News Strip Display**:
  - Removed right-side buttons (speaker/pause/refresh/close) and generic tags to dedicate 100% horizontal width to the news headline.
  - Supports up to 3 lines of full text to eliminate truncation.
  - Tapping anywhere directly on the strip speaks the headline aloud in Tamil.
  - Multi-provider feed updates (BBC Tamil, News18, Oneindia, Top News, and cybersecurity alerts).
- **Scan & Clean Button Moved Up**:
  - Placed immediately beneath the storage gauge so users can clean junk instantly without scrolling.
- **Storage Gauge with Free RAM**:
  - Shows free RAM right inside the circular storage gauge next to free storage.
- **Accurate Phone Age Calculation**:
  - Uses factory shipping API level (`ro.product.first_api_level`) and hardware/model database to calculate the true age of the device rather than the latest OS update date.
- **Streamlined AI Status Card**:
  - Reduced the large AI status card into a sleek, compact bar.
- **Instant Spoken Alerts in Tamil**:
  - Immediate charger plug-in announcement in Tamil with overheat warnings. Spoken announcements always default to clear Tamil regardless of whether English or Tamil UI is active.
