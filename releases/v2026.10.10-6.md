# Live Guard v2.32 (Release v2026.10.10-6)

### Highlights of this major update:
1. **Redesigned Status Bar Icon**:
   - Replaced generic data arrows with a modern **Live Guard Security Shield & Network Pulse symbol** (`ic_stat_data`, `ic_stat_net_gold`, `ic_stat_net_green`, `ic_stat_net_red`).
2. **Clean Status Bar Pill & LED Meters**:
   - Removed duplicate `↑↓` arrows inside the floating pill to conserve clean space.
   - Dual **[D]** and **[U]** illuminated activity bars remain prominent.
   - Direct size scaling controls (`- Smaller` / `+ Bigger`) and move pad directly on the home screen.
   - Toggle to hide mobile data count on Wi-Fi or Unlimited 5G (leaves only the active D & U lights).
   - Landscape touch-through transparency for gaming and video watching.
3. **Redesigned Home Screen Dashboard**:
   - Replaced single storage circle with a futuristic **Live Guard System Matrix**: Storage, RAM, Phone Age, Live Network Ping/Quality, and Security Score.
4. **Security Check & 100/100 Score AI Guide**:
   - One-tap "Manage" button on every flagged app/finding to quickly revoke permissions.
   - Device model detection (Xiaomi MIUI/HyperOS, Samsung One UI, Realme, OnePlus, Vivo) with step-by-step guidance to reach 100/100.
   - One-tap "Ask AI to Fix" button and Voice guide.
5. **Permissions Hub & "Allow restricted settings" Guide**:
   - Detailed walkthrough for Android 13+ and Xiaomi Security Center to unlock greyed-out restricted permissions.
   - Turn ON / Turn OFF options for Usage Access, Overlay, Storage, and Notifications.
6. **Redesigned Home-Screen Widget**:
   - Modern glassmorphism card featuring real-time mobile data, free storage, security status, and quick one-tap Clean.
