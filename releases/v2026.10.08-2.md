# CleanSweep v2.15 & Updates (2026.10.08-2)

### CleanSweep v2.15 Updates
- **Tamil News & Safety Information Strip**:
  - Removed redundant badges to maximize reading space.
  - Full multiline display prevents text cut-off / truncation.
  - Added TTS speaker button to listen to any headline aloud in Tamil.
  - 30-minute periodic background refresh and manual one-tap refresh.
- **Estimated Phone Age**:
  - Displays estimated phone age and manufacture year below the HomeScreen storage gauge.
- **Banking & UPI Apps SMS Permission Intelligence**:
  - Recognizes trusted banking, financial, and UPI apps that legitimately need SMS access for SIM verification and OTPs.
  - Prevents false high-risk malware alerts; surfaces them with weekly reminder check.
- **Charging Voice & Overheat Protection**:
  - Stabilized charging completion remaining time announcements (only announces when charging rate has settled).
  - Voice warning when device temperature is dangerously high upon plugging in.
- **12-Hour AM/PM Time Formatting**:
  - Standardized timestamps on HomeScreen, App Widget, and Settings screens to 12-hour AM/PM (`h:mm a`).
- **UI Spacing & Layout Optimization**:
  - Removed excessive blank spaces in Tamil reports and optimized daily brief formatting.
