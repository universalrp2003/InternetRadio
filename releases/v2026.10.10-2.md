# Android app updates — v2026.10.10-2

### CleanSweep v2.28
* **Zero-Waste Passive Network Quality & Speed Metering**:
  * Calculates real-time upload/download speeds, ping latency, and jitter directly from user's live traffic without burning mobile data on artificial speed tests.
  * Dynamically classifies quality for both **Wi-Fi** and **Mobile Data**.
  * Shows live network grade in the notification card and status bar overlay:
    * **Top Quality**: Gold flashing.
    * **Medium Quality**: Green fill with Gold outline.
    * **Bad / High Latency**: Red blinking.
* **Fresh News Feed & Instant Flush**:
  * News cache expires every 30 minutes.
  * Tapping the refresh button completely flushes old news and shown history, pulling 100% fresh Tamil Nadu, Indian, and international headlines immediately.
* **Accurate Wi-Fi Device Scanner**:
  * Removed disruptive auto-scan on app start and removed the 15-minute background intruder scan/announcements.
  * Expanded probing ports and ICMP reachability so all home devices (smartphones, IoT, smart TVs, PCs, printers) are reliably detected across Android 8 through Android 16+.
* **Charger Announcement Reliability**:
  * Reduced de-duplication window from 15s to 4s, ensuring quick re-plug events trigger immediately.
  * Added fallback broadcast and foreground service synchronization.
* **Realistic High-Fidelity Tamil Voice**:
  * Tuned TTS parameters (natural pitch and speed) and prioritized high-quality neural/WaveNet voices for rich, attractive, and lifelike Tamil speech.
