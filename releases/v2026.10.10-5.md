# Live Guard v2.31 (Release v2026.10.10-5)

### New in this release:
- **Dual D & U Illuminated Network Activity Lights**:
  - Integrated directly inside the expanded Status Bar Pill overlay to the right of the mobile data count.
  - **[D] Download Light Bar**: Smooth 3-segment illuminated bar lighting up from left to right.
  - **[U] Upload Light Bar**: Smooth 3-segment illuminated bar lighting up from right to left (from the data count inwards).
  - Dynamic quality coloration: **Gold** (top speed/low latency), **Emerald Green** (good quality), and **Red** (high latency/poor quality) with smooth translucent background tracks.
- **Untouchable & Transparent to Touch**: Retains zero-touch interference with the system status bar, with full drag & nudge support from Settings.
