# Live Guard v2.33 (Release v2026.10.10-7)

### Highlights of this update:
1. **Intuitive Traffic-Light Network Quality Indicators**:
   - **Very Good Quality**: Bright Green (`#00E676`) for top throughput and ultra-low ping.
   - **Medium Quality**: Warm Yellow/Amber (`#FFD600`) for moderate speed or normal latency.
   - **Bad Quality**: Alert Red (`#FF3B30`) for slow transfers, high ping (>125ms), or packet jitter.
2. **Synchronized Everywhere**:
   - Notification bar shield radar icon tints directly match current quality (Green / Yellow / Red).
   - Status bar floating pill activity LEDs and meter adapt dynamically.
   - Main screen Live Guard System Matrix highlights real-time ping and quality in matching Green, Yellow, or Red.
