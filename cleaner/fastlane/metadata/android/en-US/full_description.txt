CleanSweep is a junk cleaner and phone-health app for Android 8 and newer — Redmi and other
Xiaomi phones (HyperOS / MIUI), Samsung One UI, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, OnePlus, Pixel and stock
Android.

It looks for the things that quietly eat storage: leftover temp and residual files, thumbnail
caches, exact duplicate files, APK installers of apps you already have, folders that are about
to become empty, old downloads and very large files. Nothing is deleted on its own — every
scan is a list you review, and only the files you tick are removed. App caches are cleared
through a guided two-tap flow: CleanSweep opens the app's own storage screen, you tap "Clear
cache", you come back. There is no Accessibility service anywhere in the app, so it cannot
read your screen or tap inside other apps, and it never claims a fake "boost".

Beyond cleaning:
* Battery and hardware board — charge, temperature, voltage, current, real charging watts, CPU
  temperature and load, RAM and storage.
* Installed-app list that flags bloatware, unused apps and sideloaded apps.
* Security review — a score out of 100 with the permission and settings problems behind it.
* Wi-Fi scanner — how many devices share your network, with a best guess at what each one is
  (phone, laptop, TV, camera, printer, router). The network name is never shown or sent.
* Mobile and data screen — operator, 5G/4G, dBm signal, cell towers, public IP, ping and
  jitter, a speed test in the size you pick, and today's data usage.
* Spoken warnings — battery low, battery full, running hot, charging started, storage nearly
  full, charger pulled out early, a charger that is connected but not charging, a worn battery,
  Wi-Fi/mobile-data changes and a new device on your Wi-Fi. Every event has its own switch,
  quiet hours silence everything at night, and a once-a-day brief reads the important part out
  loud. A home-screen widget shows battery, watts, free storage and the last brief.
* The status-bar charging card shows watts, current, temperature and time to full while the
  charger is connected, and it starts by itself when you plug in.

Optional AI (off unless you turn it on): with your own key from Google Gemini, NVIDIA,
OpenRouter, Groq, OpenAI or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — or the keyless free option —
CleanSweep can explain a scan or answer a question about your phone. Nothing is sent until you
tap Analyse or Ask. With a Google key the assistant can search the web for live questions and
shows the sources it used.

The whole menu is available in English and Tamil, and the AI can answer in either language,
independently of the menu. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. CleanSweep uses the
internet only for the AI you start yourself and the speed test you start yourself.
