PulseEQ is a 20-band equalizer that does its own audio processing instead of borrowing a
system effect: peaking biquad filters, a preamp and a soft limiter, so a boosted preset does
not clip.

* 20 bands from 31 Hz to 20 kHz, with presets for music, movies, speech, podcasts, gaming and
  more — and room to move every band yourself.
* A spectrum light-bar driven by real audio energy (not a decoration), plus a built-in 20-band
  player so you can hear the difference immediately.
* Works with cooperating music players that expose their audio session, and keeps running in
  the background with a foreground service and a small notification showing the active preset.

PulseEQ uses Internet only for optional GitHub release checks. It has no ads and collects
nothing. It is an equalizer, an audio tool — it does not download music.
