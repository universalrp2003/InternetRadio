PulseEQ is a 20-band equalizer with its own digital signal processing: peaking biquad filters, a preamp and a soft limiter help prevent boosted presets from clipping.

* Twenty bands from 31 Hz to 20 kHz, with presets for music, movies, speech, podcasts, gaming and more.
* Adjust individual bands and preamp, save settings, and view the spectrum while audio plays.
* Built-in local player plus support for cooperating music players that expose an audio session. Background behavior and effects outside PulseEQ depend on Android and the device/ROM; system-wide equalization is not guaranteed.

Audio processing stays on the device. Selected audio is not uploaded. The optional GitHub release check sends ordinary request metadata to GitHub, not audio. There are no ads or developer-operated analytics.
