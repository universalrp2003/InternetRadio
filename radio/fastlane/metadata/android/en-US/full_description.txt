Ramesh Radio plays Tamil FM from Tamil Nadu, Sri Lanka, Malaysia, Singapore and the Tamil
diaspora, plus Tamil and English news radio, and your own audio files — with a real 10-band
equalizer.

* Live Tamil FM stations, grouped so you can find them quickly, with a "TN towns" chip for
  city-level FM (Madurai, Puducherry, Kodaikanal, Coimbatore, Tirunelveli and more).
* Tamil news and world English news, separated from the music stations.
* A local audio player for the music already on your phone.
* Favourites and recently played, a sleep timer, and a home-screen widget with
  play/pause and next/previous.
* A 10-band equalizer (31 Hz - 16 kHz) with Clear sound (multiband levelling and a limiter),
  rumble cut and hiss cut — so speech is clear and music has no noise.
* The station list is shipped inside the app and also searched live in the open
  Radio-Browser directory, so a station that moved can still be found.

The menu can be in Tamil or English. There are no ads, no accounts and no trackers. The app
uses the internet for one reason only: to stream the station you chose.
